About nexttext

nexttext is a lightweight toolkit for neutralizing text and generating controlled bias for testing AI pipelines. It works equally well on human-annotated training labels and on generated labels from captioning or classification models. The default path is small, inspectable, deterministic, and has zero runtime dependencies.

  • Debias — neutralize gendered or stereotypical language automatically.
  • Inject bias — inject_bias (alias rebias) reintroduces controlled skew for testing and validation.
  • Works for both training data and model outputs.
  • Simple API, no heavy dependencies.
  • Open source under the MIT license.
