About nexttext

nexttext is a lightweight Python toolkit for neutralizing and re-framing text to reduce noise, hidden skew, and unwanted artifacts in AI pipelines. It works equally well on human-annotated training labels and on generated labels from captioning or classification models.

  • Debias — neutralize gendered or stereotypical language automatically.
  • Rebias — reintroduce controlled skew for testing and validation.
  • Works for both training data and model outputs.
  • Simple API, no heavy dependencies.
  • Open source under the MIT license.
